XIAOCHEN SUN is a documentary and commercial photographer based in China.

His photographic practice focuses on architecture, hotels, hospitality spaces, interiors and the relationship between people and the spaces they inhabit.

With a background in documentary photography, he brings an observational approach to commercial space photography, paying particular attention to natural light, atmosphere, structure and human presence.

He works with hotels, restaurants, architects, interior designers and hospitality brands.
