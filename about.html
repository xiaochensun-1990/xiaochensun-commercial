Tom Sun 毕业于西南石油大学， 获地质工程硕士学位。长期从事纪实摄影与视觉创作， 作品关注地域、建筑、人的生活以及社会环境之间的关系， 并发表于国内外媒体及出版物。

在长期的纪实摄影实践中，他形成了对空间独特的观察方式： 空间不仅是建筑本身，也是人的活动、时间、光线与地方文化 共同构成的视觉现场。与单纯记录建筑形式不同， 他更关注空间所承载的气息、尺度、秩序与生活痕迹， 并尝试通过摄影建立人与建筑之间的情感联系。

近年来，他将纪实摄影的观察方法延伸至酒店、民宿、 餐厅、艺术空间及建筑项目的拍摄。在商业空间摄影中， 他注重自然光线、建筑结构、材料质感与空间氛围， 同时保留纪实摄影对于真实现场的敏感性， 使建筑不只是一个被展示的对象， 而成为具有时间感和叙事性的场所。

他的空间摄影既服务于建筑、设计与酒店行业， 也延续了其作为纪实摄影师和艺术创作者 对现实空间的长期探索。

Tom Sun holds a Master's degree in Geological Engineering from Southwest Petroleum University. He has worked extensively in documentary photography and visual art, with a practice centred on the relationship between place, architecture, people and their surrounding environments. His work has been published internationally across media and photographic publications.

His approach to space photography has grown naturally from his documentary practice. For Sun, a space is more than architecture itself; it is a visual field shaped by human activity, time, light and local culture. Rather than simply recording architectural form, he is interested in the atmosphere, scale, materiality and traces of life within a place, using photography to explore the relationship between people and the spaces they inhabit.

In recent years, he has extended this documentary approach into commercial photography, working across hotels, boutique guesthouses, restaurants, art spaces and architectural projects. He pays close attention to natural light, architectural structure, materials and atmosphere, while maintaining the observational sensitivity developed through years of documentary work.

His approach seeks to move beyond straightforward architectural representation. Through an attentive and contemporary visual language, he presents spaces as places with character, memory and narrative — images that serve the needs of architects, designers and hospitality brands while retaining the depth and authenticity of documentary photography.
