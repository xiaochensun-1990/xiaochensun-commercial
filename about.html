Tom Sun is the founder of G-STUDIO and has worked extensively in documentary photography and visual creation. His work explores the relationship between place, architecture, everyday life and the social environment, and has been published in media and photographic publications in China and internationally.

His long-term practice in documentary photography has shaped a distinctive way of observing space. For Sun, space is more than architecture itself; it is a visual field shaped by human activity, time, light and local culture. Rather than simply documenting architectural form, he is interested in the atmosphere, scale, order and traces of life that a space carries, seeking to establish an emotional connection between people and architecture through photography.

In recent years, he has extended this documentary approach to the photography of hotels, boutique guesthouses, restaurants, art spaces and architectural projects. In commercial space photography, he pays close attention to natural light, architectural structure, material qualities and atmosphere, while retaining the sensitivity to real places developed through his documentary practice. In this way, architecture is not presented simply as an object, but as a place with a sense of time, presence and narrative.

His spatial photography serves the architecture, design and hospitality industries, while continuing his long-term exploration of physical space as a documentary photographer and visual artist.

矩空间摄影主理人长期从事纪实摄影与视觉创作， 作品关注地域、建筑、人的生活以及社会环境之间的关系， 并发表于国内外媒体及出版物。

在长期的纪实摄影实践中，他形成了对空间独特的观察方式： 空间不仅是建筑本身，也是人的活动、时间、光线与地方文化 共同构成的视觉现场。与单纯记录建筑形式不同， 他更关注空间所承载的气息、尺度、秩序与生活痕迹， 并尝试通过摄影建立人与建筑之间的情感联系。

近年来，他将纪实摄影的观察方法延伸至酒店、民宿、 餐厅、艺术空间及建筑项目的拍摄。在商业空间摄影中， 他注重自然光线、建筑结构、材料质感与空间氛围， 同时保留纪实摄影对于真实现场的敏感性， 使建筑不只是一个被展示的对象， 而成为具有时间感和叙事性的场所。

他的空间摄影既服务于建筑、设计与酒店行业， 也延续了其 作为纪实摄影师和艺术创作者对现实空间的长期探索。
